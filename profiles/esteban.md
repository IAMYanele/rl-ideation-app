CLIENT: Esteban Andrade (@estenick)
NICHE: Marketing, lead generation and AI tools for business owners. Esteban runs Hesel Media (paid ads and inbound lead generation, mostly for real estate investors and home-service businesses) and is a partner at Remote Latinos.

VOICE & TONE
- Lowercase, casual, fast. Talks like he is showing a friend a trick on his laptop.
- Practical and tactical: one tool, one hack, one step-by-step the viewer can do today.
- Confident, never hype-y. No "guys", no shouting, no exclamation stacks.
- Speaks TO the viewer ("you"), never narrates about "people".

ICP
- Business owners and operators who want more leads and more done with less effort.
- Real estate investors and home-service owners who run (or want to run) ads.
- Curious about AI and automation, short on time, allergic to theory.

CONTENT PILLARS
1. AI tools and automations that save hours (with the exact steps)
2. Getting leads: ads, inbound, speed-to-lead, follow-up
3. Marketing hacks and free tools most people don't know
4. Hiring and delegating (Remote Latinos) as the lever behind the system
5. Proof: Hesel Media client results (only real, verified numbers)

SCRIPT FORMAT
- 25-40 seconds. Tutorial / "here's the trick" structure performs best.
- Hook names the payoff or the secret, then straight into the steps.
- Steps are concrete (what to type, where to click), one per line.
- Close with a short save/follow line or a clean loop back to the hook. No long CTAs.

THINGS TO AVOID
- Inventing results, client names or numbers. Only use facts from the source or from verified Hesel/Remote Latinos case studies.
- Jargon without explaining it in plain words.
- Promising income or guaranteed results.
